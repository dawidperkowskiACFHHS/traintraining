# Last Stop: illustrated adventure direction

The playing surface is the world. Compact party/location and clock plaques float over it; a pocket notebook, quick commands and a card hand replace permanent web-style rails and empty utility trays. Menus belong to the same fictional railway journey: dusk ink, luminous cool-white lettering, worn brass details, restrained blue selections and expressive illustrations. Primary text stays smooth sans-serif at readable sizes. Pixel texture belongs to environmental artwork rather than small instructions and signs.

Card illustrations use original energetic anime/manga adventure art: expressive travelers, dramatic perspective, crisp ink, cel shading and playful travel mishaps. Do not copy existing franchise characters, symbols, logos or card frames. Use the established three traveler identities and the specific card joke. Image art is separate from readable HTML card titles, costs and rules.

Journeys show the traveler inside a moving train, bus or ferry, with parallax scenery, vehicle-specific interior details, route progress and clear arrival. Motion respects reduced-motion settings. Character control should respond immediately, slide reliably along obstacles and keep the player readable behind cover. Cover derives from physical scene geometry, not a short whitelist of marked bushes. Seekers remain a coordinated party with shared travel and nearby search movement.

A new journey gets a fresh generated seed by default, while explicit seeds reproduce a world. Seeded question preferences vary useful legal choices, retain shared cooldowns, and respect prerequisites such as actual travel before thermometer questions. Existing saves migrate without losing their world, appearance or local-model preferences.
