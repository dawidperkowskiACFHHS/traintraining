# Quick-match timing experiment

Matched starting seeds across four regions and three difficulties, with automatic classic NPCs controlling all three roles. Both caps include normal deduction, card use, curse tasks, timetable waits, travel, and local searching. Subsequent choices can diverge after a cap changes the first result. These are progression and pacing checks, not human win-rate estimates.

| Search cap | Matches | Turns | Captures | Forfeits | Mean match seconds |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 240s | 12 | 39 | 20 | 0 | 1050 |
| 360s | 12 | 36 | 32 | 0 | 1050 |

New Quick journeys use a six-minute search cap. Classic remains ten minutes. Old saves keep their stored rules. Detailed stage totals in artifacts/release/balance-check.json are sampled every two game seconds; curse-active time overlaps other stages. Human deduction speed and the feel of escapes still need playtesting.
